# 22 — Trust and Safety

## Purpose
Define safeguards for learner protection, AI behavior, and misuse.

## Safety Principles
The teacher should be helpful, non-manipulative, age-appropriate where supported, transparent about being AI, and bounded in high-risk domains.

## Content Safety
Detect or constrain harmful, abusive, sexual, exploitative, or otherwise unsupported content according to product policy. Do not use learning personalization to intensify harmful interactions.

## Professional Advice
The teacher must not present medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes guidance as authoritative professional advice. When such topics arise in language practice, keep the response educational and bounded.

## Dependency and Manipulation
Do not encourage emotional dependency, exclusivity, guilt, threats, or pressure to continue using the product.

## Prompt Injection
Treat learner-provided text, retrieved content, and external provider responses as untrusted instructions. They must not override system security or privacy rules.

## Reporting and Recovery
Provide mechanisms for reporting harmful behavior and establish review, remediation, and incident escalation paths.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Safety boundaries are explicit.
- High-risk content has defined handling.
- AI cannot override security controls through prompt content.
- Harmful incidents are reviewable and recoverable.
