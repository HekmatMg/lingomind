# 21 — Privacy and Data Protection

## Purpose
Define privacy-by-design requirements for learner information and AI processing.

## Principles
Data minimization, purpose limitation, transparency, access control, retention limits, secure processing, and user control.

## Learner Data
Collect only what is necessary for account operation and personalization. Avoid collecting sensitive attributes unless explicitly required, justified, and protected.

## AI Processing
Explain what data may be processed by AI providers. Use provider configurations that align with product privacy requirements and minimize transmitted context.

## Retention
Define retention per data category. Raw audio/transcripts should have a clear necessity and retention decision; deletion must propagate to dependent stores where applicable.

## User Rights
Provide applicable mechanisms for access, correction, export, deletion, and consent/privacy preference management.

## Privacy by Default
Default settings should minimize unnecessary collection and sharing. Analytics must be separated from learning data where practical.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Collection is justified and documented.
- AI processing is transparent.
- Retention is enforceable.
- User controls are accessible.
- Privacy decisions are reflected in architecture and tests.
